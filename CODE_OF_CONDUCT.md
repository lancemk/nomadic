# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, maintainers and contributors pledge to make participation in `nomadic` a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful in disagreement
- assuming good intent while discussing technical tradeoffs
- giving actionable, constructive feedback
- showing empathy for different experience levels and backgrounds
- focusing on the work and the impact rather than the person

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- personal attacks, insults, or trolling
- publishing private information without permission
- abusive or deliberately disruptive conduct
- bad-faith participation intended to derail discussion or review

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other community interactions related to the repository.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers through GitHub rather than escalating publicly in an issue thread. If private repository security reporting is available, use it for sensitive reports; otherwise contact the maintainer account associated with the repository.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing community standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
